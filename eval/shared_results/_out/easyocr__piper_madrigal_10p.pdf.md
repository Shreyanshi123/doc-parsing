MDGL further expands pipeline with global licensing agreement with ARWR (covered by Tenthoff) for ARO-PNPLA3, clinical-stage siRNA. Additionally; MDGL highlighted the recent exclusive global licensing agreement with ARWR for the clinical-stage ARO-PNPLA3. Specifically, ARO-PNPLA3 is a GalNAc-conjugated siRNA designed to reduce expression of the PNPLA3 gene which is highly prevalent among Hispanic MASH patients and represents ~30% of moderate-to-advanced fibrosis patients (consistent with F2IF3). Accordingly, ARWR will receive an upfront S25M payment with additional payments of up-to S975M if certain milestones are achieved and royalties on net sales_ In return, MDGL is granted the exclusive global license to develop, manufacture, and commercialize ARO-PNPLA3. With this, it is crucial to appreciate ARO-PNPLA3 already showed Ph1 (NCT04844450) PoC data in n-55 MAFLD patients who were either homozygous or heterozygous carriers of the PNPLA3 [148M variant (~93% Hispanicl Latino; Fabbrini E, et al: N Engl Med. 2024 Aug 1;391(5).475-476). For reference , ARO- PNPLA3 showed (Exhibit 4): 1) up-to -46% reductions in liver fat (MRI-PDFF) at Week 12 following single-dose at the highest dose tested in PNPLA3 I148M homozygous patients; 2) rapid onset of action with reductions observed as early as Week 6 and sustained response through at-least Week 24; 3) strong safetyltolerability with no clinically meaningful AEs observed; and 4) no liver fat content effect observed in heterozygous patients at any of the doses. Ofnote, a second Ph1 (NCT05039710) in n=9 Japanese patients further support these data Hence, ARO- PNPLA3 represents a de-risked clinical-stage asset with clear potential as a precision medicine approach for patients at high-risk of MASH: When asked about what gave them confidence in ARO-PNPLA3 to move forward on this deal, mgmt discussed its high potential to provide complementary efficacy with REZDIFFRA. Specifically, MDGL highlighted their previous data which supported that if there is greater reduction in liver fat then there is greater efficacy associated with REZDIFFRA Given that ARO-PNPLA3 was able to drive up-to -46% liver fat reduction with clean safetyltolerability profile, MDGL believes there is strong potential for additive efficacy with REZDIFFRA in the context of this subpopulation. Additionally, mgmt reiterated this licensing agreement continues to support their growing R&D strategy where they also gained the global rights to six pre-clinical siRNA programs in February 2026 (preclinical development underway):
