AACR 2026: Importance rising

The American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) used to be considered a pre-clinical and early-stage clinical oncology conference with relatively low significance: However; this year it is expected to be highly important event that could lead sector rebound depending on data results, as key domestic biotech firms are set to disclose core data. Draw attention to Rznomics and HLB Innovation

Clinical data requiring particular attention includes that from: 1) Rznomics; and 2) HLB Innovation. Rznomics (RNA editing) and HLB Innovation (solid tumor CAR-T) are to present the first proof of concept for their respective platform technologies (proving efficacy and safety, respectively). Both are scheduled for oral presentations at the AACR__Rznomics on Apr 20 at 8.00 AM (KST) and HLB Innovation on Apr 21 at 2.00 (KST):

Attempt to obtain approval $ second- line treatment for bile duct cancer

Bile duct cancer is a cancer that occurs in the bile duct (bile duct; gallbladder, ampulla), the passage for bile: While the annual number of new patients in the US is relatively small at approximately 25,000, it is a cancer type with steady unmet needs The biliary tract cancer market targeted by tovecimig consists of patient groups that are:1) unresectable (90%); 2) ineligible for targeted therapy (80% vs HER2, IDHI, FGFRZ, BRAF, etc ); and 3) ECOG 0-1 (good systemic condition, 90%). 1.EASL: MASH data releases (Hanmi Pharm, D&D Pharmatech)

EASL: Hanmi and D&D

The European Association for the Study of the Liver (EASL, held in May) represents a top-tier global liver disease conference along with the American Association for the Study of the Liver (AASLD; held in November): As for this year's event; which is scheduled to be held over May 27~30, we draw attention to global MASH data release: While late-breaking abstract titles have yet to be disclosed, we predict MASH phase Il data from Hanmi Pharm (partner MSD-efinopegdutide) and D&D Pharmatech (DDOI) will be released:

1) Hanmi: Proving superiority over semiglutide

1) Hanmi Pharm's efinopegdutide: The efinopegdutide phase Ilb study (total N-381) will disclose biopsy data at week 52, revealing MASH resolution and fibrosis improvement data for the first time. The study is designed with three drug-administered cohorts and control groups consisting of a placebo and semaglutide 2.4mg (the only global MASH clinical design to directly compare with semaglutide in phase II). The primary endpoint is the MASH resolution rate, where confirming statistical superiority in direct comparison with the accelerated-approved semaglutide is most critical (semaglutide ESSENCE phase Ill: 63 at week 72, placebo-adjusted 29%). If it succeeds in proving superiority over semaglutide even in the secondary endpoint; the fibrosis improvement rate; it will be considered a definitive indicator of phase IIl success (semaglutide: 37% at week 72, placebo-adjusted 15%).

Furthermore, indirect comparisons can be made with the phase Il results of survodutide, leading GLPIGCG candidate_ We estimate MASH resolution rate at 30~40% and a fibrosis improvement rate at approximately 15% (both placebo-adjusted) The value of efinopegdutide attributable to Hanmi is estimated at WItn (W1.8tn upon success; excluded upon failure)

2) D&D: Phase IIl entry
