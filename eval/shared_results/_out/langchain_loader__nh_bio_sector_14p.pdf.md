AACR 2026:
Importance rising
The American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) used to be considered a pre-clinical
and early-stage clinical oncology conference with relatively low significance. However, this
year it is expected to be a highly important event that could lead a sector rebound
depending on data results, as key domestic biotech firms are set to disclose core data. The
conference is scheduled for Apr 17~22 (local time), and clinical abstract data is to be released
early Saturday morning (KST).
Draw attention to
Rznomics and HLB
Innovation
Clinical data requiring particular attention includes that from: 1) Rznomics; and 2) HLB
Innovation. Rznomics (RNA editing) and HLB Innovation (solid tumor CAR-T) are to present
the first proof of concept for their respective platform technologies (proving efficacy and
safety, respectively). Both are scheduled for oral presentations at the AACR—Rznomics on
Apr 20 at 8:00 AM (KST) and HLB Innovation on Apr 21 at 2:00 (KST).
1) Rznomics: ORR to
be around
low/mid-30%
range or higher
1) Rznomics: The core data is the ORR (efficacy) from the phase Ib/IIa triple combination
study of its lead pipeline RZ-001 (hTERT) for primary liver cancer. The existing first-line SoC
IMbrave150 showed a final ORR of 27% (benchmark). As this is the first ORR
announcement for RZ-001, it will involve interim data (estimated to be primarily low-
dose) rather than the full patient group (45 patients), making a direct comparison with
IMbrave somewhat ambiguous at this stage. However, confirming an ORR in the low-to-
mid 30% range or higher would be considered positive. While market expectations for
ORR have recently risen, the fact that this involves low-dose cohorts must be taken into
account.
2) HLB Innovation:
No DLT reported
2) HLB Innovation: The first validation of the KIR platform (separating activation/inhibition
signals) is expected through the SynKIR-110. Since it is a solid tumor CAR-T, toxicity is the
most critical point to watch (high affinity is required for high activity in the tumor
microenvironment, which increases the potential for toxicity compared to hematologic
CAR-T). Attention should be paid to safety indicators, as this is the first clinical data from
low-dose administration groups (Cohorts 1-3, with up to Cohort 6 planned for the future).

Tovecimg’s OS and
PFS data to be
disclosed in April
In April, Compass Therapeutics is scheduled to disclose OS and PFS data from the phase II/III
trial of tovecimig (ABL001) as a second-line treatment for biliary tract cancer. This is
significant, as South Korea's ABL Bio is the original developer of tovecimig, and tovecimig is
highly likely to become ABL's first commercialized product (expected in late 2026~2027).
Attempt to obtain
approval s second-
line treatment for bile
duct cancer
Bile duct cancer is a cancer that occurs in the bile duct (bile duct, gallbladder, ampulla), the
passage for bile. While the annual number of new patients in the US is relatively small at
approximately 25,000, it is a cancer type with steady unmet needs. The biliary tract cancer
market targeted by tovecimig consists of patient groups that are:1) unresectable (90%); 2)
ineligible for targeted therapy (80% vs HER2, IDH1, FGFR2, BRAF, etc.); and 3) ECOG 0-1 (good
systemic condition, 90%). The plan is to first obtain FDA approval as a second-line treatment,
and an expansion to first-line treatment may be attempted later depending on the results of
investigator-initiated 1L clinical trials at the MD Anderson Cancer Center.
OS ORR data
disclosed last year;
but key is OS data
In Apr 2025, top-line data (n=111) for ABL001+Paclitaxel from the US phase II/III trial
(COMPANION-002) in second-line patients ineligible for targeted therapy was disclosed
(crossover for the paclitaxel monotherapy group was permitted after disease progression).
The ORR was 17.1% (CR 0.9%, PR 16.2%; vs paclitaxel 5.3%, p=0.031) and the DCR was 61.2%,
with safety profile remaining consistent with previous studies. That said, the OS data, which
is critical for FDA drug review, is scheduled to be released in April. For reference, as the trial
permitted crossover between patient groups, rank preserving structural failure time (RPSFT)
adjustments will also be presented in the OS interpretation.
ABL Bio’s ABL001
deserves 40~50% of
Compass’s market
cap
The partner company, Compass Therapeutics, has a market cap of approximately W1.5tn
based on its core asset tovecimig. Looking at its contract structure with ABL Bio, the
remaining milestones include US$96mn for development/approval and US$300mn for
commercialization (option: US$110mn if ophthalmic indications are added), with a single-
digit royalty rate. For ABL Bio, the value of tovecimig can be recognized as the relative value
of Compass's market capitalization. Considering the milestones and the single-digit royalty
rate, reflecting around 40~50% of Compass’s market cap appears to be reasonable
(presenting the current value of tovecimig attributable to ABL Bio at approximately
W600bn~750bn). Following the data announcement in April, the value attributable to ABL
will also change according to the fluctuations in Compass's market capitalization.

1.EASL: MASH data releases (Hanmi Pharm, D&D Pharmatech)
EASL: Hanmi and

1) Hanmi: Proving
superiority over
semiglutide
The European Association for the Study of the Liver (EASL, held in May) represents a top-tier
global liver disease conference along with the American Association for the Study of the
Liver (AASLD, held in November). As for this year’s event, which is scheduled to be held over
May 27~30, we draw attention to global MASH data release. While late-breaking abstract
titles have yet to be disclosed, we predict MASH phase II data from Hanmi Pharm (partner
MSD-efinopegdutide) and D&D Pharmatech (DD01) will be released.
1) Hanmi Pharm’s efinopegdutide: The efinopegdutide phase IIb study (total N=381) will
disclose biopsy data at week 52, revealing MASH resolution and fibrosis improvement data
for the first time. The study is designed with three drug-administered cohorts and control
groups consisting of a placebo and semaglutide 2.4mg (the only global MASH clinical design
to directly compare with semaglutide in phase II). The primary endpoint is the MASH
resolution rate, where confirming statistical superiority in a direct comparison with the
accelerated-approved semaglutide is most critical (semaglutide ESSENCE phase III: 63% at
week 72, placebo-adjusted 29%). If it succeeds in proving superiority over semaglutide even
in the secondary endpoint, the fibrosis improvement rate, it will be considered a definitive
indicator of phase III success (semaglutide: 37% at week 72, placebo-adjusted 15%).

ADA: 1) survodutide
and 2) RNA-based

The American Diabetes Association (ADA) conference is a key venue where major obesity-
related data is frequently presented. This year’s event is scheduled for Jun 5~8. As some ADA
abstract titles have been released, we explore investment ideas based on survodutide and
RNA-based obesity treatments from a global perspective.
Survodutide obesity
phase III results (first
phase III outcome)
Survodutide (GLP/GCG) is a MASH/obesity pipeline under phase III trials by Boehringer
Ingelheim (original developer: Zealand Pharma), with multiple phase III results expected this
year. It is considered a global best-in-class candidate for MASH, while a separate phase III trial
is underway to explore expansion as an obesity drug. A search of this year's ADA abstract
titles confirms that the outcome of Synchronize-1 phase III trial to evaluate survodutide
for treatment of obesity will be released. This is highly significant as it marks the first phase
III results for survodutide and suggests its potential as an obesity drug beyond MASH.
Data preview:
Tolerability is key
In the previous 46-week phase II obesity trial, survodutide recorded a placebo-adjusted
weight loss of 16% at the 4.8mg (highest dose) level, but tolerability issues were raised at
high doses (4.8mg group: vomiting 35%, treatment discontinuation 29%, GI-related
discontinuation 26%). Accordingly, the pure obesity phase III trial (Synchronize-1, 46-week)
aims to improve tolerability by increasing the dose more slowly (titration) (phase II: titration
every 2 weeks from 0.3mg, 0.6mg, 0.9mg up to 4.8mg vs phase III: dose escalation every 4
weeks up to a maximum of 6mg). We foresee that the phase III data will show a weight
loss improvement of around 15% (placebo-adjusted); however, the reduction in
discontinuation rates due to tolerability issues should be the most critical point.
Noteworthy
domestic plays:
G2GBio and
Inventage Lab
In the US, peak sales for survodutide are estimated at around US$3bn~5bn (sales
assumption: obesity 50%, MASH 50%). If superior data (about 15% placebo-adjusted weight
loss + increased tolerability) is disclosed at the upcoming ADA, survodutide's peak sales
estimate may be revised up. In relation to survodutide, we suggest paying attention to
Korea’s microsphere pharmaceutical companies G2GBio and Inventage Lab. Both
companies have signed R&D agreements with Boehringer Ingelheim for monthly
formulations. While the item under the agreements remains undisclosed, it is highly likely to
be survodutide. As both companies aim to sign formal contracts with Boehringer Ingelheim
within the year, expectations could heighten if survodutide gains prominence.
