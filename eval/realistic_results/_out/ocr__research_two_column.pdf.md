Predictive Maintenance and Unplanned Downtime

Abstract. We study the effect of predictive maintenance on unplanned downtime in discrete manufacturing plants. Across twelve

plants, downtime fell by thirty one percent.

1. Introduction

Unplanned downtime remains the largest single source of lost
output in discrete manufacturing. Condition monitoring is
widely deployed but rarely linked to maintenance planning.

2. Method

We instrumented 214 machines with vibration and
temperature sensors and trained a gradient boosted model to
predict bearing failure seven days ahead.
