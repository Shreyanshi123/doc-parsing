# Standard Operating Procedure: Goods Receipt

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how incoming goods are received, inspected and booked into stock.

## 2. Procedure

1. Check the delivery note against the purchase order.
2. Inspect packaging for damage and record any damage on the delivery note.
3. Move the goods to the quarantine bay.

- Count cartons.
- Photograph any damage.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Step | Owner | Record |
|------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Receipt | Store keeper | GRN form |
| Inspection | Quality inspector | Inspection report |
| Release | Quality inspector | Stock ledger entry |
