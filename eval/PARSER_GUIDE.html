3. Good to know

  • "App picks" is the first parser Smart Extract tries for that type of file. It chooses by file type, not by the situation inside the file, so it can miss (for example two tables side by side).
  • hybrid_pdf = PyMuPDF + Tesseract OCR. smart_pdf = the same plus a vision model that reads charts and photos (max 8 per document, needs "Include paid parsers").
  • adi_vision = Azure Document Intelligence reads text and tables, a vision model reads only charts and photos (max 8 per document; never a whole page). Paid, never picked automatically. It matches the best parsers on merged-header tables and scans, and is weaker on nested tables.
  • Docling is often best but slow: minutes on documents of 30 to 100 pages.
  • Some situations rest on one or two documents; the text says so. All documents were generated by us, not real company files. Differences under 0.1 are noise.
  • Not tested: Azure Document Intelligence (paid). vlm (sends every page to the LLM) is switched off.
