# Annual Report 2026

## Chairman's message

Northstar Industries delivered a year of disciplined growth.
Revenue rose by eleven percent, helped by stronger demand
in the pumps and valves businesses, while operating cost
grew more slowly than sales.

## Strategy

Our strategy rests on three pillars: reliability of supply,
investment in automation, and closer partnerships with our
largest customers.

## Risks

Principal risks remain commodity prices, currency movements
and the pace of the warehouse migration.
