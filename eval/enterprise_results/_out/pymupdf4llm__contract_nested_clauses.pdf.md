# **MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

Agreement No. MA-2026-77, made on 1 May 2026 between Northwind Industrial Supplies Pvt Ltd and Apex Machinery Works.

## **1. Definitions**

- 1.1 "Services" means the services described in Schedule 1.

- 1.2 "Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a party.

   - 1.2.1 Control means the power to direct the management of an entity.

## **2. Term**

- 2.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for thirty six months.

- 2.2 It renews for successive twelve month periods unless either party gives ninety days notice.
