1. Summary

  • Question: which parser is best for which document, based on measured scores, not guesses.
  • Test: 62 documents first, then 43 harder ones (14 tough, 14 real-world style, 6 long: 30 to 100 pages, 3 long mixed: text, tables, charts, photos and scans in one file, 31 to 99 pages, 6 research reports from a colleague, 9 to 66 pages, answer files for key pages only). Every parser on every document, scored against a hand-written answer.
  • Finding: no parser is best for everything. Plain text and simple tables: fast parsers are enough. Charts: smart_pdf. Scans: Docling. Merged cells and nested tables: PyMuPDF4LLM, OpenDataLoader or Docling.
  • New combo, ADI + vision: Azure Document Intelligence reads text and tables, a vision model reads only charts and photos. Paid, not used by the routing. It matches the best parsers on merged-header tables and scans (4.96 vs smart_pdf 2.96 and 2.36), and adds little on the research reports (section 3).
  • Still wrong: a few documents drop to about 3.0 in Smart Extract (section 4). Nothing was changed in the routing.
  • Note: all documents are generated by us, not real company files. The 6 research reports (9 to 66 pages) come from a colleague; their answer files cover key pages only.

4. Problems found (none fixed yet)

  1. Table lines counted as a figure. clinical_trial_table 3.01 and financial_statement_multiheader 2.96 in paid By document; free route 5.0 and 4.86. PyMuPDF splits a merged-cell table into small tables, the detector sees the lines as a picture, and smart_pdf then writes the numbers twice. Confirmed by running the detector directly.
  2. By part repeats merged cells. purchase_order 3.54 vs 4.96, lab_report 3.26 vs 4.55, bill_of_lading 3.93 vs 4.56, policy_schedule_nested 3.06 vs 4.77, 100-page ledger 3.01 vs 5.0 (By part vs By document). Seen directly in the lab_report output; probable on the others.
  3. A network error looks like a bad score. annual_report_spread first scored 3.98 because the chart call failed; rerun gave 4.53.
  4. The rule is per document type, not per file. On payslip the choice (OpenDataLoader) scored 3.94, Docling 4.69.
  5. hybrid_pdf and smart_pdf are weak on scans (2.3 to 2.7 vs Docling 4.4 to 4.7). Smart Extract avoids this by using Docling for scans.
  6. Native Word and Excel readers lose a little (page header dropped, list numbers flattened).
  7. Long report with charts: PyMuPDF4LLM is best at text and tables but reads no charts; smart_pdf reads charts but is weaker on text and tables. Both end at 4.18.
  8. Long mixed documents are slow with smart_pdf and By part. On the 36-page report with 6 scanned pages, smart_pdf did not finish in 10 minutes and By part did not finish in 15 (cause not investigated; an orphaned Python process was also running on the laptop). By document with PyMuPDF4LLM took about 3 to 4 minutes and scored 4.64, close to the best (Docling 4.66, 7 minutes). On the 31-page tender pack the paid route (smart_pdf, 3.77) scored below the free one (PyMuPDF4LLM, 3.90) and Docling (4.07). On the 99-page file the paid By document run (smart_pdf, 3.66) was also below the free one (PyMuPDF4LLM, 3.93), and By part took 13 minutes.
  9. Only 8 figures per document are read by the vision model. The 99-page report has about 27 charts, so smart_pdf scored only 1.1 on charts (others 0.0), and the 56-page report with 10 charts is also capped. For long documents with many charts most charts stay unread.

5. Done and not done

  • Done: 62-document test, routing built from it, three harder test sets, causes of problems 1 and 3 found.
  • Not done: fixes for the problems above (they change routing, need your yes), Azure Document Intelligence on the new sets (paid), real client documents.
  • Limits: generated documents, one run each, differences under 0.1 are noise, timings are rough.

What this shows. On the 9 documents that were run both ways, the free parsers scored higher on 5 (04_scientific_research_paper, 10_mixed_parser_killer, datasheet_with_photo, flowchart_doc, product_brochure) and the paid vision path on 2 (01_corporate_annual_report, annual_report_page).
