# Hot Work Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out the controls required before any welding or cutting is carried out.

## 2. Requirements

- Obtain a hot work permit
- Remove flammable material within 10 metres
- Keep a fire extinguisher at the work area

## 3. Steps

1. Inspect the area
2. Issue the permit
3. Monitor for 30 minutes after work
