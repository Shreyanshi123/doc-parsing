# Options for sharing tax computation sheet

Employees can either view the sheet and save it on their system or can send/forward it to an Email ID (personal/official).

## Option 1

To view your tax calculation sheet, follow the steps mentioned below.

1. Enter your PAN (in caps).
2. Select “Financial period ID”.
3. Click on “View”.
4. Click on “OK”.

Your Income Tax Calculation sheet will appear

## Option 2

To email the tax calculation sheet, follow the steps mentioned below.

1. Enter your PAN (in caps).
2. Select “Financial period ID”.
3. Click on “send email” and enter your email address.
4. Click on “OK”.

The system will send the tax calculation sheet on the email address with a copy to your Evalueserve email address. After sending the email, the system will show the following message.
