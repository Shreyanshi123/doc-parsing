## 1. Investment sentiment affected (Kosdaq bellwether stocks: Alteogen, Samchundang Pharm)

Domestic healthcare stocks performed sluggishly

In 1Q26, healthcare stocks performed somewhat sluggishly (Kospi +39% vs Kospi Pharma - 3%; Kosdaq +20% vs Kosdaq Pharma +2%). In particular, it was disappointing that the biotech sector, which had high expectations due to policies aimed at revitalizing the Kosdaq market, performed worse than expected. The overall sluggishness of the sector was largely influenced by reliability issues arising from Kosdaq bellwether stocks.

A series of reliability issues have emerged at sector bellwether stocks

In the Kosdaq biotech sector, when a leading stock vacates its position, a new momentum stock frequently takes its place. For instance, after the merger and dissolution of the Kosdaq leader Celltrion Healthcare between 2020 and 2023, Alteogen took over the leading position in 2024 following the disclosure of its revised contract with MSD. Furthermore, when Alteogen's share price plummeted in early 2026 due to royalty issues, Samchundang Pharm rose to the top spot on the Kosdaq through the disclosure of its SPass contract. However, issues regarding business/clinical strategy and patents have been raised. With consecutive blows to the reliability of leading stocks in 1Q26, investor sentiment in the sector has frozen.

## 2.Bio stock rebound scenarios for 2Q26

Two momentums: L/O deals and data release at conferences

Major momentums are essential for domestic bio stocks to make a rebound, and we believe large-scale L/O deals and conference data release could serve as main drivers behind the industry rebound in 2Q26. 2Q is traditionally a peak season for the pharmaceutical and bio sector, and a large-scale sector rebound is possible through the realization of the two major momentums.

Large L/O deals: Focus on LigaChem and Hanmi

Key momentum is summarized in the following table. LigaChem Bio and Hanmi Pharm are expected to sign mega-scale L/O deals worth trillions of won in 2Q26~3Q26, and other major biotechs, such as Alteogen, OliX, Qurient, ABL Bio, HK inno.N, and TiumBio, are also promising.

Major conferences to commence this weekend (AACR)

As the AACR conference begins this weekend, with multiple late-season conferences (EASL, ASCO, and ADA) scheduled for May-June, we present detailed previews of major global conference data and related investment ideas. We also list 2Q26 data presentation momentum from both domestic and global perspectives (including Eli Lilly’s orforglipron prescription, Compass’s ABL001, and Novartis’ Pelacarsen). As the pharma/biotech sector enters its peak season in 2Q26, we recommend staying well-informed on the table below.

Top picks for 2Q26: Hanmi Pharm and Rznomics

We maintain Hanmi Pharm and Rznomics as our top picks for 2Q26, drawing attention to: 1) expectations for licensing-out deals for obesity drug and EASL-MASH phase II data for Hanmi and the disclosure of the first efficacy data at the AACR; and 2) RNA-based Alzheimer's disease treatment trend in 2H26 for Rznomics. For other plays with momentum as well, we see investment merit.

## II. April: Biopharma sector to enter peak momentum season

## 1. AACR to begin this weekend

AACR 2026: Importance rising

The American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) used to be considered a pre-clinical and early-stage clinical oncology conference with relatively low significance. However, this year it is expected to be a highly important event that could lead a sector rebound depending on data results, as key domestic biotech firms are set to disclose core data. The conference is scheduled for Apr 17~22 (local time), and clinical abstract data is to be released early Saturday morning (KST).

Draw attention to Rznomics and HLB Innovation

Clinical data requiring particular attention includes that from: 1) Rznomics; and 2) HLB Innovation. Rznomics (RNA editing) and HLB Innovation (solid tumor CAR-T) are to present the first proof of concept for their respective platform technologies (proving efficacy and safety, respectively). Both are scheduled for oral presentations at the AACR—Rznomics on Apr 20 at 8:00 AM (KST) and HLB Innovation on Apr 21 at 2:00 (KST).

1) Rznomics: ORR to be around low/mid-30% range or higher

1) Rznomics: The core data is the ORR (efficacy) from the phase Ib/IIa triple combination study of its lead pipeline RZ-001 (hTERT) for primary liver cancer. The existing first-line SoC IMbrave150 showed a final ORR of 27% (benchmark). As this is the first ORR announcement for RZ-001, it will involve interim data (estimated to be primarily low- dose) rather than the full patient group (45 patients), making a direct comparison with IMbrave somewhat ambiguous at this stage. However, confirming an ORR in the low-to- mid 30% range or higher would be considered positive. While market expectations for ORR have recently risen, the fact that this involves low-dose cohorts must be taken into account.

2) HLB Innovation: No DLT reported

2) HLB Innovation: The first validation of the KIR platform (separating activation/inhibition signals) is expected through the SynKIR-110. Since it is a solid tumor CAR-T, toxicity is the most critical point to watch (high affinity is required for high activity in the tumor microenvironment, which increases the potential for toxicity compared to hematologic CAR-T). Attention should be paid to safety indicators, as this is the first clinical data from low-dose administration groups (Cohorts 1-3, with up to Cohort 6 planned for the future).

### 3) Y-Biologics: Next x2 Keytruda

Meanwhile, among South Korean companies presenting pre-clinical data, we recommend drawing attention to Y-Biologics. While pre-clinical abstracts were pre-released on Mar 18, core data will be disclosed on the day of the poster presentation. Y-Biologics plans to present mouse data posters for: 1) AR170 (PD-1xIL-2xVEGF); and 2) AR166 (PD-1xLAG3xIL-2) on Apr 22 at 1:00 AM (KST).

### Ivonescimab (next- generation Keytruda): FDA approval is key

The most important agenda in global anti-cancer drug development this year is whether the next-generation Keytruda (ivonescimab, PD-1xVEGF) receives FDA approval (HARMONi: PDUFA goal date set for Nov 14, 2026). Additionally, immature PFS data for HARMONi-3 (2Q26), immature OS for HARMONi-6 and final OS for HARMONi-2 (mid-2026), and final PFS and immature OS for HARMONi-3 (2H26) are scheduled for release throughout the year. From a data perspective, HARMONi-3 is significant, as it represents the first global head-to- head comparison data against Keytruda (corresponding to China's HARMONi-6).

### Development of next-generation ivonescimab also underway

Meanwhile, China has already moved beyond next-generation Keytruda (PD-1xVEGF) and is already developing the next generation of ivonescimab (trispecific antibody: PD-1xVEGFx new target). Regarding next-generation ivonescimab, CStone’s CS2009 (PD-1xVEGFxCTLA4) is being developed at the fastest pace, with phase I/II data disclosed in China last March. Safety was favorable with Grade 3 or higher TRAE of 23%, and efficacy was excellent with an ORR of 90% in 1L PD-L1 TPS >50 NSCLC (vs competitors' PD-1xVEGF in the 70% range).

### AR170’s early data competitiveness against ivonescimab

From the perspective of next-generation ivonescimab, we draw attention to the pre-clinical safety and efficacy data of Y-Biologics’ AR170 (PD-1xIL-2xVEGF). As a trispecific antibody that also targets IL-2 (differing from CStone’s CTLA4 target), competitive early data compared to ivonescimab is expected.

Attempt to obtain approval s second- line treatment for bile duct cancer

Bile duct cancer is a cancer that occurs in the bile duct (bile duct, gallbladder, ampulla), the passage for bile. While the annual number of new patients in the US is relatively small at approximately 25,000, it is a cancer type with steady unmet needs. The biliary tract cancer market targeted by tovecimig consists of patient groups that are:1) unresectable (90%); 2) ineligible for targeted therapy (80% vs HER2, IDH1, FGFR2, BRAF, etc.); and 3) ECOG 0-1 (good systemic condition, 90%). The plan is to first obtain FDA approval as a second-line treatment, and an expansion to first-line treatment may be attempted later depending on the results of investigator-initiated 1L clinical trials at the MD Anderson Cancer Center.

OS ORR data disclosed last year; but key is OS data

Survodutide obesity phase III results (first phase III outcome)

Survodutide (GLP/GCG) is a MASH/obesity pipeline under phase III trials by Boehringer Ingelheim (original developer: Zealand Pharma), with multiple phase III results expected this year. It is considered a global best-in-class candidate for MASH, while a separate phase III trial is underway to explore expansion as an obesity drug. A search of this year's ADA abstract titles confirms that the outcome of Synchronize-1 phase III trial to evaluate survodutide for treatment of obesity will be released. This is highly significant as it marks the first phase III results for survodutide and suggests its potential as an obesity drug beyond MASH.

Data preview: Tolerability is key

In the previous 46-week phase II obesity trial, survodutide recorded a placebo-adjusted weight loss of 16% at the 4.8mg (highest dose) level, but tolerability issues were raised at high doses (4.8mg group: vomiting 35%, treatment discontinuation 29%, GI-related discontinuation 26%). Accordingly, the pure obesity phase III trial (Synchronize-1, 46-week) aims to improve tolerability by increasing the dose more slowly (titration) (phase II: titration every 2 weeks from 0.3mg, 0.6mg, 0.9mg up to 4.8mg vs phase III: dose escalation every 4 weeks up to a maximum of 6mg).
