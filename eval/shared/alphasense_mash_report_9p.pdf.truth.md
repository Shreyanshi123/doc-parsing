Market Overview and Epidemiology
The MASH market represents a multi-blockbuster opportunity driven by a global surge in obesity and metabolic dysregulation. Industry estimates suggest that MASLD affects approximately 30-38% of the adult population worldwide, with prevalence in Europe reaching 25-30%.

US Patient Population and Diagnosis
- Diagnosed Population: As of year-end 2025, there are approximately 680,000 diagnosed F2/F3 MASH patients in the US, reflecting nearly 50% growth from 2023 estimates.
- Advanced Disease: There are approximately 245,000 addressable US patients with F4 compensated cirrhosis (F4c), with 42x greater risk of liver-related mortality than earlier stages.
- Diagnosis Rates: Only about 10% of MASH patients are currently diagnosed.

Competitive Landscape and Commercial Dynamics
- Madrigal Pharmaceuticals (Rezdiffra): over 23,000 patients on therapy as of 2Q25. Management is targeting approximately 315,000 diagnosed F2/F3 patients currently under specialist care.
- Novo Nordisk (Wegovy): Approved for MASH by the FDA in August 2025; its share of newly written prescriptions rose from 4% to 21% between November 2024 and November 2025.
- Eli Lilly (Zepbound/Tirzepatide): Phase 2 data showed MASH resolution rates as high as 62% in the 15mg dose group.
