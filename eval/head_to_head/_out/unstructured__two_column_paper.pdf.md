## Bearing Fault Diagnosis with Combined FeaturesA. Rao and S. Iyer

- 1. IntroductionCondition monitoring of rotating machinery relies onvibration signals. In this study we compare three featuresets on a bearing fault data set. The ﬁrst feature setuses time domain statistics such as root mean squareand kurtosis. The second uses spectral band energies.

- 2. Method and resultsThe third feature set combines both with envelopeanalysis. A random forest classiﬁer achieved the bestaccuracy on the held out test set. Results aresummarised in Table 1. We conclude that combinedfeatures give the most robust diagnosis acrossoperating speeds.
