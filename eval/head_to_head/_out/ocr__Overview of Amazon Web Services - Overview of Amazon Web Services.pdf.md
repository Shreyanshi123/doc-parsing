Introduction

In 2006, Amazon Web Services (AWS) began
offering IT infrastructure services to businesses as
web services—now commonly known as cloud
computing. One of the key benefits of cloud
computing is the opportunity to replace upfront
capital infrastructure expenses with low variable
costs that scale with your business. With the
cloud, businesses no longer need to plan for and
procure servers and other IT infrastructure weeks
or months in advance. Instead, they can instantly
spin up hundreds or thousands of servers in
minutes and deliver results faster.
